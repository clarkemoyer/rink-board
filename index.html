Tap a song and it plays from its start time in the player above, fades over the last half second, and stops at its end time. Tap another song to switch instantly, or Stop to cut it. On iPhone the very first play may need one tap on the video itself.

This page has to be served over https (GitHub Pages, Netlify, your own site). Opened straight from a file on disk, YouTube refuses to play it with error 153.

Cuts marked "est." are starting points; open Edit cuts to nudge them a second at a time. Your changes are saved in this browser; use Share setup to copy them to another phone or laptop.

On a keyboard, the key shown on each pad plays it and Space stops.
